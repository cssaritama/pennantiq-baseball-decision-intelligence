# PennantIQ Product Definition

PennantIQ is a Baseball Data Intelligence and Decision Memory Platform. The public prototype supports pregame pitcher preparation, contextual exploration, matchup planning, chronological Shadow Mode and postgame organizational learning.

The current product must not be described as a complete digital twin. The long-term vision is a Championship Decision Twin that connects team-owned data, scenario simulation, organization-specific models and continuous learning.

The first user is an advance scouting and pitching strategy analyst. The product supports human judgment and abstains when evidence is insufficient.
