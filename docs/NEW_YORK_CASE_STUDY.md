# New York Decision Intelligence Lab

## Purpose

Use New York as the flagship public proving ground without implying affiliation with the Yankees, Mets or MLB.

PennantIQ is team-agnostic. New York is the story, not the market boundary.

## Two complementary public cases

### The Bronx Championship Lab

Research question:

> How can a contender improve the quality and repeatability of marginal decisions that matter in October?

Focus areas:

- starter preparation;
- opponent matchup plans;
- bullpen sequencing roadmap;
- evidence quality;
- adaptation detection;
- pregame decision discipline;
- postgame learning.

### The Queens Turnaround Lab

Research question:

> How can an organization diagnose where expected strength is not converting into results and preserve the reasoning behind corrective decisions?

Focus areas:

- recent-form diagnosis;
- context and opponent effects;
- roster/role questions in the roadmap;
- decision replay;
- uncertainty and evidence gaps;
- learning across losing and winning stretches.

## Included real snapshot

The repository contains final game results from July 1–21, 2026:

- Yankees: 8–7 in the included window;
- Mets: 7–9 in the included window.

This small snapshot is intentionally limited. It demonstrates a real Command Center input but does not diagnose either organization and is not used as pitch-level evidence.

## What a real historical case requires

Before each historical game cutoff:

- bounded Statcast data available before the game;
- probable starter / roster information;
- recent appearances and rest;
- opponent handedness and expected lineup information when timestamp-valid;
- park/environment context;
- versioned PennantIQ decision package.

After the game:

- observed decisions and outcomes;
- execution versus preparation;
- public context that changed;
- evidence/process review;
- preserved lesson.

## Questions PennantIQ should support

- How is this starter arriving across his last five appearances?
- Is the velocity or whiff change stable or context-driven?
- Which hitter-plan combinations have enough evidence?
- Does home/away, handedness, rest or venue change the evidence?
- What should we do with a debutant or sparse-history pitcher?
- Where do specialist agents disagree?
- What did the organization believe before the game?
- What actually happened?
- What should the organization update?

## Strategic mission

> **Bring October glory back to New York—one better decision at a time.**

This is the narrative mission. The measurable product objective is preparation quality, evidence traceability, calibrated uncertainty and organizational learning.
