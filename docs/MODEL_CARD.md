# Model Card

## Public baseline
The strategy ranker is an empirical-Bayes descriptive baseline. It groups comparable events by pitch family and zone, computes mean defensive value and shrinks small samples toward a global prior.

## Intended use
Pregame research, education, portfolio demonstration and historical shadow evaluation.

## Not intended
Causal claims, autonomous pitch calling, in-game use without rule review, medical decisions or championship prediction.

## Known limitations
Selection bias, missing private context, pitch-command feasibility, catcher and coach intent, small samples, era changes, source corrections and the gap between historical association and intervention effect.
