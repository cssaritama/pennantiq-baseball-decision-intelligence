# Dashboard and Interface Specification

## 1. Command Center

Audience: front office, director and lead analyst.

Required cards:

- data freshness;
- records, games and players;
- source health;
- evidence and abstention rates;
- recent team trajectory;
- open decisions awaiting postgame review.

## 2. Starter Pulse

Audience: pitching coach and advance analyst.

Required visuals:

- velocity by appearance;
- whiff and hard-contact trends;
- pitch mix and entropy;
- rest and role context;
- last starts table;
- evidence/limitations panel;
- debutant protocol when activated.

## 3. Matchup Lab

- pitcher and batter selector;
- count and context;
- Plan A / Plan B / Avoid;
- sample size and posterior value;
- adaptation signal;
- evidence package;
- human approval action.

## 4. Context Matrix

- selectable split dimension;
- shrunk ranking;
- home/away × handedness heatmap;
- sample and game counts;
- confounding warnings;
- stability status.

## 5. Shadow Mode

- evaluation coverage;
- abstention;
- historical match rate;
- chronological trace;
- leakage policy;
- associative-not-causal warning.

## 6. Decision Ritual

- pregame decision form;
- evidence strength;
- human confidence;
- open/closed learning loops;
- postgame outcome and lesson;
- searchable memory.

## 7. Trust and Monitoring

Minimum charts:

1. queries over time;
2. latency;
3. provider usage;
4. feedback;
5. evidence strength;
6. abstention;
7. grounded-answer signal;
8. source freshness in production.

## Visual direction

- deep navy, ivory and restrained gold;
- red only for risk;
- no MLB logos or false affiliation;
- cinematic but calm;
- critical numbers visible before decorative graphics;
- every recommendation one click from its evidence.
