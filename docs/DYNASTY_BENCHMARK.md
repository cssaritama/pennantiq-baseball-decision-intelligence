# Dynasty Benchmark

## Why it exists

Winning once can contain noise. Winning repeatedly is a stronger organizational signal.

The Dynasty Benchmark asks:

> Which publicly observable patterns persist in teams that win again?

## Anchor cases

- **2024–2025 Los Angeles Dodgers:** back-to-back World Series champions; the first repeat champion since the 1998–2000 Yankees era ended.
- **1998–2000 New York Yankees:** three consecutive championships.

These cases are inspiration and external benchmarks, not proprietary templates.

## Candidate dimensions

- regular-season run differential;
- run prevention;
- offensive depth;
- roster availability and depth;
- platoon flexibility;
- starting-pitching and bullpen leverage performance;
- replacement-level resilience;
- trade-deadline additions;
- postseason leverage execution;
- player-development contributions;
- year-over-year roster continuity and adaptation.

## Scientific boundary

Public outcomes cannot reveal the internal causal mechanism of a club. The benchmark therefore separates:

1. observed public performance;
2. statistical associations;
3. hypotheses about repeatability;
4. private organizational mechanisms, which remain unknown unless provided by the organization.

## Future output

A `Dynasty Readiness Map` should show where an organization resembles or differs from historical repeat champions while avoiding a single magic score.
