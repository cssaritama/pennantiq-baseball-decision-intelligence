# Baseball Decision Taxonomy

PennantIQ begins with one narrow decision surface and expands only after validation.

## Implemented wedge
### Pitcher–batter pre-game planning
- Pitch-family and zone scenarios by count.
- Evidence strength and abstention.
- Opponent adaptation signals.
- Shadow Mode replay.

## Expansion surfaces
### Pitching staff
- Starter plan, times-through-order signals and bullpen availability.
- Reliever matchup sequencing and workload constraints.

### Offense
- Hitter approach, swing-decision zones and pinch-hit scenarios.
- Lineup construction under uncertainty.

### Defense and baserunning
- Positioning scenarios, throwing decisions and run-expectancy tradeoffs.
- Steal, advance and send/hold decisions.

### Player development
- Repertoire design, command development and measurable intervention tracking.

### Front office
- Roster fit, option/transaction scenarios and organizational depth.

### Governance
- Pregame/in-game/postgame capability controls, audit logs and model-risk review.

Each module needs its own user, decision, data contract, baseline, evaluation design and responsible-use boundary. A folder name is not an implemented feature.
