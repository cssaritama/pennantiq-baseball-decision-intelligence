# A Day in the Life of PennantIQ

## 05:00 — Data close

Scheduled jobs ingest the previous day's permitted data, validate schemas, version snapshots and update governed feature tables.

## 06:00 — Overnight learning

Shadow Mode closes yesterday's decisions, compares the pregame evidence with the observed result and flags learning opportunities. No future information is allowed into the historical recommendation snapshot.

## 07:00 — Morning Intelligence Brief

The Decision Council prepares:

- probable-starter pulse;
- opponent adaptation signals;
- matchup evidence;
- bullpen availability inputs when the module is enabled;
- data-quality warnings;
- unresolved questions for human scouting.

## 09:00 — Analyst War Room

The analyst explores what changed, challenges the strongest signals, adds human scouting context and records uncertainty.

## 12:00 — Scenario preparation

PennantIQ builds Plan A / Plan B / Avoid packages for expected matchups. Sparse evidence triggers abstention instead of fabricated precision.

## Pregame — Decision commit

Approved strategy artifacts are frozen with timestamps, source versions, evidence strength and human ownership.

## In-game

PennantIQ respects current MLB technology rules. The commercial vision is not an unauthorized autonomous dugout strategist. Operational use must remain within league and club policy.

## Postgame — After Action Review

PennantIQ replays:

- what was known;
- what was believed;
- what was chosen;
- what occurred;
- what should change.

## Weekly — Organizational learning

Patterns across decisions become searchable memory rather than disappearing inside one analyst's notebook.
