# Competitive Landscape

PennantIQ must not claim that “nobody else uses AI in baseball.” That is false and commercially weak.

## Established categories

### Tracking / measurement

TrackMan provides professional baseball tracking and describes in-game, player-development and scouting use. PennantIQ should integrate measurement providers rather than pretend to replace the hardware layer.

### Analytics / video / scouting workflow

TruMedia provides MLB and NCAA teams with data, video, custom reports and proprietary-data integration. PennantIQ should not compete by being “another filterable scouting dashboard.”

### Team-internal analytics

MLB clubs maintain private models, scouting systems and decision processes that are not publicly visible. Absence of a public product is not proof that a capability does not exist internally.

### Emerging agentic baseball products

Public vendors have begun marketing autonomous or multi-agent scouting/analytics. Therefore **multi-agent architecture alone is not a moat**.

## PennantIQ differentiation hypothesis

PennantIQ should compete on the **decision-learning loop**, not raw data access:

1. point-in-time evidence;
2. specialist decision council;
3. explicit abstention;
4. Shadow Mode historical replay;
5. decision memory;
6. organizational learning;
7. rule-aware governance;
8. public/private plugin boundary;
9. evaluation of agent trajectories and decisions, not just generated reports.

## Partnership posture

Potential providers are better viewed as integration partners:

- tracking systems feed governed evidence;
- video platforms provide review context;
- cloud providers supply scalable infrastructure;
- PennantIQ supplies the decision orchestration, audit and learning layer.

## Commercial test

A buyer should be able to answer:

> Why should we add PennantIQ if we already have TrackMan, TruMedia and internal analysts?

The product is not ready for enterprise sales until the answer is supported by pilot evidence such as faster preparation, higher evidence coverage, better calibration or more complete decision-memory capture.
