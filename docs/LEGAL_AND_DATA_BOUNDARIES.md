# Legal and Data Boundaries

This document is an engineering control, not legal advice.

- The repository code is open source under its stated license.
- Synthetic demo data may be redistributed with the repository.
- Real sports data must be used according to the source's current terms and applicable agreements.
- Current-data adapters download into ignored local directories.
- Team names may be used descriptively in independent analysis; official logos and affiliation claims are excluded.
- A commercial edition requires licensed or team-owned data, access control and a reviewed data-processing agreement.
- Player health or biometric modules remain suspended until consent, governance and medical boundaries are established.
