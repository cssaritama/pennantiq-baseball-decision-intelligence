# MLB AI Boundary — July 2026

## What changed

In July 2026, MLB made team custom tabs on league-provided dugout iPads inaccessible after those tabs had expanded beyond their original purpose and, in some cases, included recommendations about substitutions, pitch calling and other in-game decisions traditionally made by players and coaches.

This is **not** a general prohibition on baseball analytics or AI research. It is a strong signal that product design must respect the boundary between preparation technology and automated in-game strategic direction.

## PennantIQ operating boundary

### Core commercial surface

- pre-series intelligence;
- pregame preparation;
- between-game adjustments;
- postgame replay;
- player development;
- roster research;
- organizational memory;
- analyst workflow and evidence governance.

### Restricted/sensitive surface

PennantIQ does not ship an autonomous dugout pitch-calling or substitution recommender in the public product.

Any future in-game functionality must undergo current-rule, CBA, device-policy and legal review before deployment.

## Opportunity created by the restriction

The restriction strengthens two product categories:

1. **PennantIQ Team:** deeper preparation before the game so the human arrives with a robust plan rather than needing a prohibited live copilot.
2. **PennantIQ Integrity:** audit logs, model governance, time-based feature controls and competitive-integrity policies for leagues and clubs.

## Product principle

> Prepare deeply before the game. Preserve human agency during the game. Learn relentlessly after it.
