# Notices and Data Attribution

PennantIQ code and synthetic demo data were created for this independent research repository.

## MLB and club marks

Major League Baseball, club names and related marks belong to their respective owners. PennantIQ is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by MLB or any club.

The repository does not redistribute a large MLB-derived pitch dataset. Optional Statcast connectors let an operator retrieve data locally after reviewing current MLB/Baseball Savant terms. Files downloaded into `data/real/` are ignored by Git by default.

## Frozen game-result snapshot

`data/frozen_real/new_york_results_2026.csv` is a small factual game-result snapshot assembled on July 22, 2026 for application demonstration. It is not pitch-level evidence and carries no affiliation claim.

## Retrosheet

The repository includes a connector but no bundled Retrosheet dataset. Retrosheet currently requires the following statement for transfers of its data or products developed from it:

> The information used here was obtained free of  
> charge from and is copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested  
> parties may contact Retrosheet at 20 Sunset Rd.,  
> Newark, DE 19711.

The connector writes this notice beside downloaded files. Verify the current Retrosheet notice before use.
